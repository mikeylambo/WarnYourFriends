| 2026-11-10 | **Stride length is derived, not set.** v0.3 §10's table gives stride × cadence = 5.0 / 8.4 / 13.8 m/s, but §7–9 keeps speeds at 6 / 10 / 15. With the feet locked to distance, cadence = speed ÷ stride, so only two can hold. Kept: the speeds (explicitly unchanged) and the cadence band (tested). Cadence is interpolated by speed between knots at each tier's top speed (2.0 / 2.1 / 2.3) and clamped to 2.0–2.3; stride = speed ÷ cadence, i.e. 3.0 / 4.8 / 6.5 m at top speed. The table's stride values are kept in `tuning.ts` as `specStrideLength` for reference. | Feet can't slide and cadence can't leave the band; the speeds were explicitly unchanged. | Mike's feel check: if the Jog stride (3.0 m vs 2.5 m) reads too long, lower Jog top speed rather than raising cadence |
| 2026-11-10 | **Wide Side yaw is clamped by the LOCKED screen-up law.** At 52° pitch a 35° yaw shows travel ~42° off screen-up. Every angle's yaw is limited to atan(tan(35° − 4° margin) × sin(pitch)) (Wide Side → ~25°), and the measured on-screen angle is enforced every frame (orbit toward behind until inside the law). Measured max 34.5° over 720k frames. | LOCKED beats provisional. | If Mike wants a wider side look, raise Wide Side's pitch, not its yaw |

| 2026-11-10 | Unsmashable hit: −1 tier, 0.3 s stumble, speed ×0.55 (floor 2), knocked back to the lane the change started from; head-on with no recent change → the previous lane if clear, else the nearest clear lane (knock-back takes 0.22 s). No second penalty while already reeling. | v0.3 §7–9; "previous lane" needs a rule for head-on hits. | — |
| 2026-11-10 | Walls: a lane input into a wall is a 0.9 m bump out and back over the tier's lane-change time, no stumble, no tier loss. A merge closing the giant's lane pushes it into the next lane the same way. | v0.3 "walls and barriers bounce you back into the lane, no stumble". | — |
| 2026-11-10 | Smash-across uses the same class rule as forward hits (Stride smashes Medium, Rampage Heavy, Shoulder Check +1 class) for anything the giant's footprint sweeps during a lane change. | v0.3 §7–9. | — |
| 2026-11-10 | Highway: 40 m segments, heading eased within each (smoothstep, so curvature has no kinks), curves 3–8°, a merge every 6–11 segments (lane count ±1, 2–4), starts at 4 lanes ("Highway 4"), first 3 segments straight and prop-free. Curves are biased back toward the start heading so the road meanders. Merges are telegraphed with a warning sign 1–2 segments ahead and chevrons in the closing lane. | v0.3 §16 / §40. | Phase 4 adds the other segment types |
| 2026-11-10 | Props in lanes: rows every 16–34 m, 85% chance per row, Medium/Heavy weight 0.55/0.45, at most ⅔ of lanes per row and always ≥1 lane open; nothing placed in a lane that closes within the row. | 2026-11-10 | **Run cycle implementation:** the gait (`src/anim/gait.ts`, pure math) pins each planted foot in world space and draws legs with two-bone IK. Body drops over 1 frame at contact, holds 2 frames, then rises into the hang. | 2026-11-10 | **Camera rework: oblique news-chopper shot (GDD §15 revised at Mike's request).** Pitch 48°, FOV 35, 25° side offset that swings to the outside of a turn only after >2s of sustained turning (2.5s ease), look-ahead `speed × 1.6s`, distance 30/38/50m by tier eased over 0.8s, lag 0.35s position / 0.2s look, wobble ±0.3° @ 0.4 Hz (off under Reduced Motion), climbs over buildings rather than cutting through. | 2026-11-10 | **Look-ahead is capped by measured framing.** At 48°/FOV 35 and the §15 distances, a full 1.6s look-ahead puts the giant at ~0.95 of screen height at Stride and Rampage (bottom edge), so the look-ahead is pulled in whenever the giant's measured screen position goes below 0.85 (`giantMaxScreenY`). A closed-form cap was tried first and missed by 0.1 because of the position/look lag at speed, so the cap is now feedback from the actual projection. Measured on long straights: Jog uses the full 9.6m lead (giant at 0.80, 4.3s of road visible); Stride ~13m (0.85, 3.4s); Rampage ~18m (0.85, 3.0s). | "Giant in the lower third" and "~2s of road ahead" are the goals; 1.6s is the means. | Mike's feel check |
| 2026-11-10 | **Stick and touch steering are now camera-relative**, and mouse steering aims at the ground point under the cursor. §8 says "absolute direction", which assumed a north-up camera; with a rotating chopper shot, "up" on the stick means "into the shot". | Input must match what the player sees. | 2026-11-10 | Line-of-sight test uses building and wall boxes (plus 1.6m for parapet and roof units), checked from the camera to the giant's head. | 2026-11-10 | **Live build "doesn't start": cause and fix.** The live deploy served exactly the local build (same asset hashes, all 200, correct MIME types; `base: './'` paths fine), and it started in headless Chromium on desktop click, mobile tap and keyboard. The one failure that reproduces Mike's symptom is a browser without WebGL 2 (hardware acceleration off or a blocklisted GPU; current Chrome no longer falls back to software WebGL). Then `new THREE.WebGLRenderer()` threw at the top level of `main.ts` before any input handler was bound: the HTML and title card loaded, the start button did nothing, and nothing told the player why. **Fix:** `main.ts` is now a bootstrap that checks for WebGL 2 and then dynamically imports `game.ts`. A missing WebGL shows a "WebGL is switched off" card with steps, and any other boot error shows on a "Technical difficulties" card. Audio unlock can no longer block the start. **Regression check:** the E2E smoke runs against the production build (`vite preview` of `dist/`, or `E2E_URL=` for a deployed URL). It asserts no failed or 4xx requests and no page errors, that clicking/tapping the real `#start` button and pressing a key each start the sim, and that a no-WebGL Chromium gets the message instead of an inert title. **Not confirmed:** Mike's machine wasn't inspected. If the live URL still won't start for him, the next data point is his browser console and chrome://gpu. | This was the only reproducible way the shipped build failed to start, and it failed silently. | When Mike reports back |
