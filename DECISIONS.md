# Decisions

Build-time decisions, working defaults for open questions (GDD §46), and deviations from the GDD. Newest first. Design decisions made by Mike are in GDD §47.

| Date | Decision | Why | Revisit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-10 | **Camera rework: oblique news-chopper shot (GDD §15 revised at Mike's request).** Pitch 48°, FOV 35, 25° side offset that swings to the outside of a turn only after >2s of sustained turning (2.5s ease), look-ahead `speed × 1.6s`, distance 30/38/50m by tier eased over 0.8s, lag 0.35s position / 0.2s look, wobble ±0.3° @ 0.4 Hz (off under Reduced Motion), climbs over buildings rather than cutting through. Shake and tier pull-out apply on top. | A broadcast shot that reads the street ahead; the top-down rig showed ~1s of road at Rampage. | Mike's feel check |
| 2026-11-10 | **Look-ahead is capped by measured framing.** At 48°/FOV 35 and the §15 distances, a full 1.6s look-ahead puts the giant at ~0.95 of screen height at Stride and Rampage (bottom edge), so the look-ahead is pulled in whenever the giant's measured screen position goes below 0.85 (`giantMaxScreenY`). A closed-form cap was tried first and missed by 0.1 because of the position/look lag at speed, so the cap is now feedback from the actual projection. Measured on long straights: Jog uses the full 9.6m lead (giant at 0.80, 4.3s of road visible); Stride ~13m (0.85, 3.4s); Rampage ~18m (0.85, 3.0s). | "Giant in the lower third" and "~2s of road ahead" are the goals; 1.6s is the means. | Mike's feel check |
| 2026-11-10 | **Stick and touch steering are now camera-relative**, and mouse steering aims at the ground point under the cursor. §8 says "absolute direction", which assumed a north-up camera; with a rotating chopper shot, "up" on the stick means "into the shot". | Input must match what the player sees. A stick held to one side now keeps turning as the camera follows, like most chase cameras. | Mike's feel check (gamepad) |
| 2026-11-10 | Line-of-sight test uses building and wall boxes (plus 1.6m for parapet and roof units), checked from the camera to the giant's head. The camera climbs in 2m steps (up to 60m) with a fast rise (0.15s) and a slow settle (0.8s). The E2E bot run asserts the camera never enters a building and that a building hides the giant in under 5% of samples. Measured: 0 inside, 4/154 hidden (brief, while climbing). | "Never clip into buildings." | — |
| 2026-11-10 | **Live build "doesn't start": cause and fix.** The live deploy served exactly the local build (same asset hashes, all 200, correct MIME types; `base: './'` paths fine), and it started in headless Chromium on desktop click, mobile tap and keyboard. The one failure that reproduces Mike's symptom is a browser without WebGL 2 (hardware acceleration off or a blocklisted GPU; current Chrome no longer falls back to software WebGL). Then `new THREE.WebGLRenderer()` threw at the top level of `main.ts` before any input handler was bound: the HTML and title card loaded, the start button did nothing, and nothing told the player why. **Fix:** `main.ts` is now a bootstrap that checks for WebGL 2 and then dynamically imports `game.ts`. A missing WebGL shows a "WebGL is switched off" card with steps, and any other boot error shows on a "Technical difficulties" card. Audio unlock can no longer block the start. **Regression check:** the E2E smoke runs against the production build (`vite preview` of `dist/`, or `E2E_URL=` for a deployed URL). It asserts no failed or 4xx requests and no page errors, that clicking/tapping the real `#start` button and pressing a key each start the sim, and that a no-WebGL Chromium gets the message instead of an inert title. **Not confirmed:** Mike's machine wasn't inspected. If the live URL still won't start for him, the next data point is his browser console and chrome://gpu. | This was the only reproducible way the shipped build failed to start, and it failed silently. | When Mike reports back |

| 2026-11-09 | **Sharp-turn tier drop also triggers on a steering yank.** With §9 turn rates the literal rule (net heading change >110° within 0.5s) is effectively unreachable (Stride max ~70°, Rampage ~42° in 0.5s). So an absolute steering demand (stick, mouse, touch) more than 110° off the facing also drops one tier, once, re-arming when the aim returns within 60°. The literal heading rule is still implemented. | Keeps "every corner is a risk/reward call" (Concept) alive: a U-turn request at Rampage costs a tier; a 90° corner doesn't. | Mike's feel check |
| 2026-11-09 | **Grazing contact is a scrape, not an impact.** Hitting an unsmashable thing at under ~17° (normal component < 0.3) slides along it with a small speed loss, no tier drop, no stumble. Anything steeper is a full bounce (−1 tier, 0.3s stumble, keep 55% speed). | Brushing a wall at Rampage shouldn't feel like a crash; "never a full stop" (§10). | Mike's feel check |
| 2026-11-09 | Tier-up timer pauses (doesn't reset) when you drop below top speed from drifting; it resets on brake, bounce or tier change. | "Tiers build by running uninterrupted" while not punishing a gentle curve twice. | Mike's feel check |
| 2026-11-09 | Added per-tier **grip** (drift) and **turn lag** to `tuning.ts`. §9 gives turn rate only; "Rampage: very wide, drifty" and "slightly late on turns" need these two numbers. Drifting also scrubs a little speed. | Feel words in §9/Concept need knobs. | Mike's feel check |
