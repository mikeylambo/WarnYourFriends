# Trademark search: "Giant at Large" / "Warn Your Friends"

Preliminary web search, 2026-11-25. **Not a legal clearance.** It finds obvious conflicts only. Before a commercial
launch (store listing, paid release), run the official searches below, or have a trademark attorney do a clearance search.

## Result: no conflict found

| Search | Finding |
|---|---|
| "Giant at Large" as a trademark | No mark with that phrase. Results were unrelated GIANT marks: Ahold Delhaize's GIANT supermarkets (class 35), tax software, a supplement. Different goods; none in games (class 9 / 41). |
| "Giant at Large" as a game | No game with that title. Nearest giant-themed games: *Giants Uprising* (2021), *Giants: Citizen Kabuto* (2000), *Giant Rush* (Switch). Different names. |
| "Warn Your Friends" as a game/app mark | None found. The nearest risk pattern is Zynga's "WITH FRIENDS" marks (*Words With Friends*), which Zynga has enforced against "___ With Friends" apps. "Warn Your Friends" doesn't use that construction, so low risk, but it's the one to keep in mind. |

## Do before launch (free, about 20 minutes)
1. **USPTO Trademark Search** (tmsearch.uspto.gov): search `"GIANT AT LARGE"` and `"WARN YOUR FRIENDS"`, then `GIANT AT LARGE` without quotes, in classes **009** (downloadable games) and **041** (online games). Look at live and pending marks.
2. **App Store and Google Play:** search both titles.
3. **Steam, itch.io:** search both titles.
4. **Domains and socials:** check `giantatlarge.com` and the handles you'd want.
5. Optional: file your own mark in classes 9 and 41 once you're sure of the name (USPTO base fee is per class).
