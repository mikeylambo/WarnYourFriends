## Highway chase chopper shots (shared 2026-11-10, four stills)

The case for the lane-runner pivot (GDD v0.3). All four are night-time news-chopper footage of police chases on multi-lane highways.

- **High oblique, looking down the carriageway:** 3–4 lanes, dashed lane lines strong, cruisers in a column with light bars. The road runs up the frame.
- **Long-lens side angle on an elevated highway:** overhead gantry signs, a few cars strung out; flattening telephoto look. (Long Lens angle.)
- **Wide side angle with a "BREAKING NEWS" lower third and a station bug:** chase plus crash on a divided road; the broadcast chrome is the joke's frame. (Wide Side angle; §21 HUD.)
- **High wide on a curving highway:** cruisers boxing a suspect vehicle across lanes. (Pursuers boxing lanes off, later phases.)

Take: direction of travel up the screen, lanes readable from height, light bars as the brightest marks, a lower-third chyron. Note: the references are at night, but GDD §23's sun-bleached day look is unchanged in Phase 0b. A night broadcast look is an open question for Mike.
